# Hello: one idea per line

A twelve-second sketch for trying Motion Director: the template film with a title you can edit.

- **Purpose:** show the order of work (brief, storyboard, motion, sound) on something small.
- **Length and format:** 12 s, landscape 16:9.
- **Sound:** a soft tap as the title lands; nothing else.

## Visual direction

- White stage, the default brand's ink and aqua; one card in the middle of the frame.
- Calm and even: one idea on screen at a time, eased moves, no hard cuts.

## Beats

| Time | What happens | Title |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4 s | The title rests above the empty card. | One idea per line. |
| 4–8 s | The title clears; the card is the turn. | |
| 8–12 s | The card fills with aqua: the payoff. | |

## Hits

The title arriving; the card filling.

## Ending

The aqua card, held for a beat.
